The Idea Behind My Work

More Than Fish Production

My interest in aquaculture goes beyond producing fish. I am equally interested in exploring how fish can be processed and transformed into different food products.

This interest inspired me to experiment with different ingredients and food preparation methods to create products that bring together nutrition, creativity and convenience.

From fish cookies to soy fish pie and fish-based snacks, I enjoy exploring possibilities that make fish more versatile and introduce it in familiar and interesting ways.

I believe there is more to discover in the aquaculture value chain, especially through fish processing and product innovation.

Sharing What I Know

Fish Cookie Production Training at LASU

In September 2025, I facilitated a fish-cookie production training for university students from different parts of Nigeria during an aquaculture event at Lagos State University (LASU).

The session provided an opportunity to demonstrate how fish can be incorporated into baked products and to share practical knowledge about fish value addition.

It was a meaningful experience that strengthened my interest in teaching, practical demonstrations and sharing ideas within the aquaculture sector.
