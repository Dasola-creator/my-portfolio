About My Background

A Passion for Aquaculture and Value Addition

I am an Aquaculture graduate with an interest in fish production, processing, research and the development of innovative fish-based food products.

My academic background and practical experiences have helped me develop an understanding of different aspects of aquaculture, from fish production and management to processing and product development.

I am particularly interested in exploring ways to add value to fish, improve its utilisation and create products that appeal to different consumers.

My goal is to contribute to a more productive and innovative aquaculture value chain.
